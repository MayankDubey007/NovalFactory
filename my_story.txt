The Lost Kingdom

Chapter 1: The Beginning

Sarah had always been drawn to old maps. Her grandmother's attic was filled with them—faded parchments and leather-bound atlases that whispered of forgotten places. But none of them captivated her quite like the mysterious map she discovered tucked behind a loose brick in the fireplace.

It showed a kingdom that didn't appear in any history book. Mountains ringed in gold, forests marked with tiny silver trees, and at the center, a city with spiraling towers. The words "Luminar" were written in elegant script across the top.

She couldn't explain why, but something in her chest told her it was real.

That night, Sarah made a decision that would change everything. She would find it. She would find Luminar.

Chapter 2: The Journey Begins

Three months of research led her to an old library in the mountains of Northern Scotland. An elderly librarian named Mr. Kearns took one look at her map and went pale.

"Where did you find this?" he whispered.

"Does it mean something?" Sarah asked, her heart racing.

"It means," he said carefully, "that you're either very brave or very foolish. My family has protected the secret of Luminar for generations. Only those with the map can find it. Only those pure of heart can enter it."

He led her to an ancient book, its pages brittle with age. As she read, the legend unfolded: Luminar was a realm between worlds, hidden from those who would exploit it. It could only be reached by one who sought truth, not treasure.

Sarah looked at the old man. "I don't want riches. I just want to know it's real."

He nodded slowly. "Then perhaps you are ready."
